1. Introduction

At UdhaarAI, accessible as an Android application, your privacy and financial security are our foremost commitments. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding information collection, handling, processing, and security in strict compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, India).

2. Information Collection & Permissions

Data / Permission Purpose & Usage Storage & Security
Audio / Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) Used only when the merchant taps the mic button to speak transaction or billing commands in Hindi/English. Processed on-device or via secure transcription. Audio is never permanently recorded or sold.
Camera (CAMERA) Optional capture of customer profile picture or paper bill. Stored compressed on device; synced only to the shop's encrypted database.
Customer Ledger & Contacts Managing customer names, phone numbers, debit/credit records, and balance calculations. Stored in sandboxed local device storage and synced over TLS 1.3 to private MongoDB.
Account Passwords & PINs Securing merchant access to shop records. Irreversibly hashed using 256-bit SHA-256 cryptographic standards. Never stored in plaintext.

3. 100% Offline-First Architecture

UdhaarAI is engineered with an offline-first architecture. All customer records, ledger calculations, and transaction entries work completely without an active internet connection. When your device connects to the internet, background synchronization automatically backs up your data to your private cloud database.

4. Zero Data Selling & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your financial records, customer details, or personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You can edit or delete any customer account, product, or transaction record at any time within the app. To request complete account and data deletion from our servers, send an email to support@udhaarai.in with your registered mobile number.
